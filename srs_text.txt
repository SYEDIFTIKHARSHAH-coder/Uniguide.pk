Section 1 — Introduction
1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is to define the functional and non-functional requirements of UniGuid.pk, a web-based university admission and scholarship guidance platform developed to simplify the higher education admission process for students across Pakistan.
The system provides students with a centralized platform where they can search universities, compare academic programs, explore scholarships, check admission eligibility, and apply for admissions through a single portal.
For partner universities, UniGuid.pk allows direct submission of student applications through secure API integration. For non-partner universities, the system redirects students to the university's official admission portal.
This document serves as the primary reference for developers, testers, supervisors, stakeholders, and future maintainers throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

1.2 Scope
UniGuid.pk is a web application designed to reduce the complexity students face when applying to universities.
The platform enables students to:
Register and manage their academic profile. 
Upload educational documents once. 
Search universities throughout Pakistan. 
Compare universities, degree programs, and tuition fees. 
Explore scholarships and eligibility criteria. 
View admission schedules and deadlines. 
Apply online through UniGuid.pk for partner universities. 
Redirect to official university admission portals for non-partner universities. 
Track admission application status. 
Receive admission notifications and updates.

1.3 Objectives
The primary objectives of UniGuid.pk are:

Guide students in selecting suitable universities.

2.1 Product Perspective
UniGuid.pk is a centralized web-based platform designed to simplify the university admission process for students across Pakistan. The system provides a single platform where students can search universities, compare programs, explore scholarships, check admission eligibility, and apply for admissions.

The system supports two admission models:
Partner Universities
Students submit applications directly through UniGuid.pk. 
Applications are securely transferred to the university through API integration. 
Non-Partner Universities
Students can explore admission details. 
Clicking Apply Now redirects the student to the university's official admission portal.

3.14 Public Pages Module
FR-051 About Us Page
Requirement: The system shall display a static, publicly accessible About Us page presenting UniGuid.pk's mission, vision, and team, without requiring login.
Acceptance Criteria:
Page is reachable from the main navigation menu on every screen.
Page content (mission, vision, team) is displayed in full without truncation.
Page renders correctly on desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): The page shall fully load within 3 seconds under normal network conditions.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Content shall be organized under clearly labeled headings for readability.

FR-052 Contact Us Page
Requirement: The system shall provide a Contact Us page containing a contact form (Name, Email, Subject, Message) and the platform's official support email/phone, and shall confirm successful submission to the user.
Acceptance Criteria:
Form rejects submission if a required field is empty and shows an inline error message.
A confirmation message or reference number is shown after successful submission.
Submitted inquiries are stored and retrievable by the Admin.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Form submission shall be acknowledged within 2 seconds.
Quality Attribute – Security (NFR-004): All submitted input shall be validated and sanitized before storage.

FR-053 FAQ Page
Requirement: The system shall display a publicly accessible FAQ page listing common questions and answers relevant to guests and students, grouped by category.
Acceptance Criteria:
FAQ entries are grouped under at least one category (e.g., Admissions, Scholarships, Account).
Each question can be expanded/collapsed independently.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Users shall be able to locate a relevant FAQ entry within 2 clicks of arriving on the page.

FR-054 Privacy Policy Page
Requirement: The system shall display a Privacy Policy page describing what user data is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected, in accordance with applicable data-protection practice.
Acceptance Criteria:
Page states data collection purpose, storage location/provider, and user rights.
Page includes the last-updated date of the policy.
Quality Attribute – Compliance/Privacy (NFR-012): Content shall accurately reflect the data-handling practices described in Section 5.13 (Privacy).

FR-055 Terms & Conditions Page
Requirement: The system shall display a Terms & Conditions page describing the rules governing use of the platform, and shall require acceptance of these terms during student registration (see FR-007).
Acceptance Criteria:
Page is linked from the registration form.
Registration form shall not proceed until the user has explicitly checked an "I agree to the Terms & Conditions" checkbox.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Terms shall be presented in plain, unambiguous language avoiding legal jargon where possible.


3.15 Extended Student Profile Module
FR-056 Contact Information
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to add and edit the following contact details: Mobile Number, Alternate Contact Number, WhatsApp Number, and Personal Email.
Acceptance Criteria:
Mobile Number and Alternate Contact Number shall be validated as an 11-digit Pakistani mobile format.
Personal Email shall be validated for correct email format before saving.
Changes are persisted immediately and reflected the next time the profile is viewed.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Profile update shall be saved and confirmed within 2 seconds.
Quality Attribute – Data Integrity (NFR-010): Invalid formats shall be rejected with a clear inline error message before saving.

FR-057 Parent/Guardian Information
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to add Father Name, Guardian Name, Guardian Occupation, and Guardian Contact Number to their profile.
Acceptance Criteria:
All four fields are optional but, if provided, contact number shall be validated for correct phone format.
Saved values shall be displayed on the student's profile summary.
Quality Attribute – Privacy (NFR-012): Guardian information shall only be visible to the student, the reviewing university, and the Admin.

FR-058 Educational History
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to record their School Name, College Name, Board, Passing Year, and Grades/Marks/GPA for each prior level of education.
Acceptance Criteria:
Passing Year shall be restricted to a valid 4-digit year not later than the current year.
Grades/Marks shall be validated as a numeric percentage or GPA value within an accepted range.
A student may add more than one educational record (e.g., Matric and Intermediate).
Quality Attribute – Data Integrity (NFR-010): Numeric fields (year, marks/GPA) shall be validated before being saved.

FR-059 Skills
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to add, edit, and remove a list of technical and soft skills on their profile.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each skill entry is limited to 50 characters.
Duplicate skill entries shall be prevented.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Adding a skill shall require no more than 2 user actions (enter text, confirm).

FR-060 Language Proficiency
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to specify a proficiency level (Basic, Intermediate, Fluent, Native) for each language they select, including English and Urdu.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each language entry requires both a language name and a proficiency level before it can be saved.
A student may add multiple languages.

FR-061 Achievements
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to add academic and extracurricular achievements, each with a title, description, and optional supporting document/certificate upload.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each achievement entry requires a title.
Uploaded certificate files follow the same format/size rules defined in FR-013/FR-066.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): Achievement records shall not be lost once saved, consistent with NFR-003.

FR-062 Extracurricular Activities
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to add co-curricular and extracurricular activities, each with an activity name, role/position, and duration.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each entry requires an activity name.
Students may add, edit, or delete individual activity entries.

FR-063 Profile Completion Progress
Requirement: The system shall calculate and display, as a percentage, how much of the student's profile (personal, contact, academic, documents, skills) has been completed, updated in real time as the student edits their profile.
Acceptance Criteria:
Percentage shall recalculate immediately whenever a profile section is saved.
Sections still requiring completion shall be visibly indicated to the student (e.g., a checklist).
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): The completion percentage shall update within 1 second of a profile save.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Incomplete sections shall be clearly flagged to guide the student.


3.16 Extended Document Management Module
FR-064 Upload Transcript
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to upload their academic transcript as a PDF or image file, store it securely in Firebase Storage, and associate it with the student's profile.
Acceptance Criteria:
Only PDF, JPG, and PNG formats up to 5 MB shall be accepted.
Upload progress shall be visibly indicated to the student.
A successfully uploaded transcript shall be listed under the student's Documents section.
Quality Attribute – Security (NFR-004): Uploaded transcripts shall be stored securely and accessible only to the student, the relevant university, and the Admin.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Upload of a file up to 5 MB shall complete within 10 seconds under normal network conditions.

FR-065 Upload Domicile
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to upload their domicile certificate as a PDF or image file, store it securely, and associate it with the student's profile.
Acceptance Criteria:
Only PDF, JPG, and PNG formats up to 5 MB shall be accepted.
The uploaded file shall be listed under the student's Documents section with an upload timestamp.
Quality Attribute – Security (NFR-004): Domicile documents shall be stored securely, per Section 5.5 (Security).

FR-066 Upload Other Documents
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to upload additional supporting documents not covered by FR-013, FR-064, or FR-065, with a student-provided label for each document.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each uploaded document requires a non-empty label (e.g., "Character Certificate").
Only PDF, JPG, and PNG formats up to 5 MB shall be accepted per file.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): Uploaded documents shall remain retrievable and shall not be lost once confirmed as uploaded.

FR-067 Document Verification Status
Requirement: The system shall display a verification status of Pending, Verified, or Rejected for every document uploaded by the student, updated by an authorized reviewer (University Administrator or Admin).
Acceptance Criteria:
Newly uploaded documents default to "Pending" status.
Status changes made by a reviewer shall be visible to the student within the same session or upon next page load.
A "Rejected" status shall require the reviewer to provide a reason, visible to the student.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Status updates shall be reflected to the student within 5 seconds of the reviewer's action.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Status shall be shown using clear, color-coded labels (e.g., green = Verified, red = Rejected).


3.17 Extended University Explorer Module
FR-068 Departments
Requirement: The system shall display the list of departments/faculties offered by each university on that university's details page.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each department listed shall link to its associated programs (see FR-018).
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Department list shall load together with the university details page within 3 seconds.

FR-069 Merit Criteria

Acceptance Criteria:
Merit criteria shall specify the exact weightage percentage assigned to each component.
Where a university has no published merit formula, the system shall display "Not Available" rather than leaving the section blank.

FR-070 Campus Facilities
Requirement: The system shall display the campus facilities available at each university, including Hostel, Library, Laboratories, Sports Complex, and Transport.
Acceptance Criteria:
Each facility shall be shown as available or not available for the given university.

Requirement: The system shall display a photo gallery of campus images for each university, viewable by guests and students without login.
Acceptance Criteria:
Gallery shall support viewing at least 5 images per university where available.

Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Gallery images shall be optimized so the page loads within 3 seconds on a standard broadband connection.

Acceptance Criteria:

FR-073 University Comparison

Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Comparison data for up to 3 universities shall load within 3 seconds.


3.18 Extended Scholarship Module
FR-074 Scholarship Eligibility Checker
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to check their eligibility for a specific scholarship by automatically comparing the student's stored academic profile (marks/GPA) against the scholarship's published eligibility criteria, and shall display an "Eligible" or "Not Eligible" result with the reason.
Acceptance Criteria:
Eligibility result shall be calculated using the student's most recently saved academic data.
If a student's profile is incomplete, the system shall prompt the student to complete the required fields before calculating eligibility.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Eligibility result shall be displayed within 2 seconds of the request.
Quality Attribute – Data Integrity (NFR-010): Eligibility logic shall use validated numeric data only.

FR-075 Scholarship Deadlines
Requirement: The system shall display the application deadline for each scholarship and shall visually flag scholarships whose deadline is within 7 days.
Acceptance Criteria:
Deadlines shall be displayed in a consistent date format (DD-MM-YYYY) across the platform.
Scholarships past their deadline shall be labeled "Closed" and excluded from active listings by default.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): Deadline flags shall be recalculated daily to remain accurate.

FR-076 Save Scholarship
Requirement: The system shall allow an authenticated student to bookmark (save) a scholarship for later application, and to view and remove saved scholarships from a dedicated "Saved Scholarships" list.
Acceptance Criteria:
A saved scholarship shall persist across sessions until explicitly removed by the student.
The save/unsave action shall be available directly from the scholarship listing and details pages.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): The save/unsave action shall confirm within 1 second.


3.19 Extended Admission Module
FR-077 Admission Calendar
Requirement: The system shall display a consolidated calendar view of all open, upcoming, and closed admissions across universities, showing key dates in chronological order.
Acceptance Criteria:
Calendar shall allow filtering by month and by university.
Each calendar entry shall link directly to the corresponding admission details (FR-023).
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Calendar shall load within 3 seconds for the default (current month) view.
Quality Attribute – Usability (NFR-005): Open, upcoming, and closed admissions shall be visually distinguished (e.g., by color).

FR-078 Application Fee Payment
Requirement: The system shall allow a student to pay the required application fee online through an integrated, secure payment gateway before an internal admission application (FR-025) is marked as submitted.
Acceptance Criteria:
Payment status shall be recorded as Pending, Paid, or Failed.
An application shall not be forwarded to the university until payment status is "Paid".
A payment receipt/reference number shall be issued to the student on successful payment.
Quality Attribute – Security (NFR-004): Payment transactions shall be processed over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and shall not store raw card data on UniGuid.pk servers.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): A failed payment shall not create a duplicate or partially-submitted application record.

FR-079 Application Submission Confirmation
Requirement: The system shall generate and display a unique application reference number and confirmation message immediately after a student successfully submits an internal admission application.
Acceptance Criteria:
Reference number shall be unique per application.
Confirmation shall also be recorded in the student's Application History (FR-030).
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Confirmation shall be displayed within 3 seconds of submission.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): A confirmed submission shall never be lost, consistent with NFR-003.

Acceptance Criteria:

Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Notifications shall be generated within 5 minutes of the triggering event.

FR-105 Scholarship Notifications
Requirement: The system shall notify a student when a new scholarship matching their profile, or a scholarship they have saved, becomes available or is updated.
Acceptance Criteria:
Notification shall include the scholarship name and a summary of the update.
Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Notifications shall be generated within 5 minutes of the triggering event.

FR-106 Deadline Reminders

Acceptance Criteria:

Reminder shall clearly state the exact deadline date and the item it refers to.

Quality Attribute – Performance (NFR-001): Status change notifications shall be delivered within 5 minutes of the status update.
Quality Attribute – Reliability (NFR-003): A status-change notification shall never be silently dropped.

Quality Attribute – Security (NFR-004): Only authenticated Admin accounts shall be permitted to send system-wide notifications.

12.6 Data Integrity Rules

Every application references a valid student and program.

Universities can only access applications submitted to their own institution. 
Firestore Security Rules enforce role-based access.

Appendix B – Project Scope
UniGuid.pk is a centralized university admission and scholarship guidance platform for Pakistan. The system enables students to search universities, explore programs and scholarships, view admission status, and apply online to partner universities or be redirected to official university admission portals for non-partner institutions.

Appendix C – Assumptions
Users have internet access. 
Firebase services are available. 
Partner universities provide valid API endpoints. 
University data is kept up to date. 
Students provide accurate information.
